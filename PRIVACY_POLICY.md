# Privacy Policy for NutriDecide

**Effective Date:** March 23, 2026

NutriDecide ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your health and personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Health DNA Profile:** To provide personalized food analysis, we collect data such as Age, Gender, Height, Weight, and medical conditions (e.g., Diabetes, Hypertension, PCOS).
- **Scan History:** We store a history of your scanned food products locally and securely in the cloud to provide health trends.
- **Authentication Data:** We use Firebase Auth to manage your account and synchronize your profile across devices.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- **Personalized Analysis:** Your health data is used locally by our inference engine to determine food suitability.
- **Health Trends:** We visualize your scan history to help you identify behavioral patterns (e.g., "Sunday Sodium Spikes").
- **Cloud Sync:** We use Cloud Firestore to ensure your health profile is available on all your devices.

## 3. Data Safety & Security
- **Encryption:** All data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS.
- **Access Control:** We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure that ONLY you can read or write your personal health profile.
- **Offline First:** Much of our analysis happens locally on your device to minimize data exposure.

## 4. No Medical Advice
NutriDecide provides nutritional guidance and is NOT a medical diagnostic tool. You should always consult with a healthcare professional for clinical decisions.

## 5. Your Choices
You can update or delete your health profile at any time within the app settings.
